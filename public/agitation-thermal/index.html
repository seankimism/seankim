Interpretation

  • Numerical verification checks these equations and conservation; no experimental equipment validation is claimed.
  • Agitation off removes the imposed global-mixing term; conduction and an assumed wall-driven natural circulation remain.
  • Prescribed 5/10/30 s mixing times in thermal studies are independent inputs; only the bolus study recomputes t95 from fixed RPM at each fill.
  • The liquid-side film coefficient stays at 300 W/(m² K) across mixing conditions to isolate internal redistribution; these studies do not infer agitation-dependent film transfer or mechanical heating.
  • 7,680 cells is neither the smallest nor finest tested mesh. Both 15,360-cell directional reference layouts are retained; their disagreement is not experimental uncertainty.
  • The 0.1/0.25/0.5 °C and 1/2/5% criteria are study choices. Measured cost/error picks are machine- and horizon-specific, not absolute optima or proof of local field convergence.
  • The 120-cell feed fields are illustrative. Their no-agitation warm-up behavior cannot establish convergence or replace the fine fixed-fill results.
  • PI regulates an ideal boundary temperature, with 20–40 °C limits, not heater electrical power or the vendor's internal control hardware.
  • The changing-volume Applikon 5 s cases approach 36.5 °C from below and never start the exact-trigger feed; final volume remains 1.6 L.
  • No cell-based continuous-feed videos for XDR or Applikon are present. Bolus feeds were run for AMBR and XDR only.
  • Detailed inlet jets, cold-plume descent, momentum, and azimuthal gradients are not resolved. Display averaging in 7,680-cell videos can soften local extrema.
