Interpretation and assumptions

SOUR is 5.5 pmol/cell/day; VCD follows the published NISTCHO standard-feed spin-tube profile. Liquid volumes are fixed. Net uptake is assigned to the sparger gas, with negligible liquid O₂/N₂ storage, no N₂ consumption and no separate surface-exchange term. Gas flows use dry 0 °C and 101,325 Pa; headspaces are well mixed at 35.5 °C and 101,325 Pa. The pressure-controlled vent carries the current headspace composition. O₂ utilisation is an assumed gas-use efficiency, not a prediction from bubble size, agitation or liquid kLa. These trajectories demonstrate gas accounting; they do not establish adequate oxygen transfer or culture performance.
