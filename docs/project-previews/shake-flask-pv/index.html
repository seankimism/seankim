Incubator-to-incubator RCF calculator

During tech transfer, a shake-flask process may move between incubators with different orbital diameters. Copying the same rpm changes the nominal acceleration when the orbit changes. This calculator translates the transferring site's established speed into a receiving-site rpm with equal orbital relative centrifugal force (RCF, ×g). It provides a clear, documented starting point for comparing equipment settings and planning transfer studies, alongside confirmation of vessel conditions and culture performance.

Use the installed full orbit diameter, not the flask diameter or orbit radius. Replace the example values with your equipment specifications.
